Bio

My name is Сatrina a.k.a. Сatunic. I’m a UX/UI designer, specializing in creating digital products & experiences, brand identities, and interactions. Driven by my passion for design, business, and technology I try to learn and discover new things every day to create unique visuals and experiences.

With each project, I push my work to new horizons, always putting quality first. I love crafting effective visual solutions that delight users and help businesses grow.

Experience

  • CSSSR

    I worked on projects, focusing on developing and optimizing user interfaces while implementing new functionality.

    • MadCurve — I was responsible for both UX and UI design, ensuring seamless, user-friendly, and visually appealing experiences by conducting in-depth UX research, analyzing user needs, performing usability testing, creating information architecture and interactive prototypes, developing visual concepts for web, and enhancing the component library and design system to standardize interface solutions.
    • Gazprom — I worked on complex enterprise solutions, optimizing digital tools for internal users by conducting UX research to improve workflows and enhance usability for professionals in the energy sector, designing intuitive dashboards and data visualization tools for analytics, ensuring consistency with corporate brand guidelines and industry best practices.

    Both roles required a strong focus on user-centric design, balancing aesthetics with functionality to create efficient and engaging digital experiences.

  • Neoteam

    I have designed UX/UI for global brands, enhancing digital products while preserving their identity. 

    • GLS — I revamped the delivery service, making it more user-friendly and increasing engagement. 
    • Brisa — I improved an app for people with multiple sclerosis, adding intuitive features while maintaining brand consistency. 
    • BestDrive — I designed a website with seamless service booking and an integrated online store. 
    • Bentley — I upgraded the German website, refining the interface to attract new customers. 
    • Medion — I developed personalized recommendations and optimized customer support, boosting sales and customer loyalty. 
    • BMW Bank — I streamlined navigation for a more intuitive experience.

    Across all projects, I focused on usability, business goals, and visual aesthetics.

  • Warmintro

    I led the modernization of the platform, refining its design, introducing new features, and enhancing the user interface to create a more engaging and user-friendly social network. Working closely with the project team, I ensured a seamless implementation that aligned with both user needs and business objectives. Through extensive research, prototyping, and testing, I optimized the user experience, making navigation more intuitive and interactions more fluid. The updated platform not only improved usability but also strengthened brand identity. As a result, user feedback was overwhelmingly positive, leading to increased engagement, higher retention rates, and a more vibrant online community.

  • Fartca

    My primary objective was to develop a user-friendly business platform that enables companies to offer promotions and discounts to their customers effectively. To achieve this, I designed a new corporate identity that aligned with the platform’s vision, ensuring a cohesive and recognizable brand presence. I also developed the core service for distributing and redeeming coupons, allowing users to easily access and utilize promotional offers. Additionally, I created a personal account system for distributors, streamlining the management of promotions and customer interactions. Through extensive research, iterative design, and close collaboration with stakeholders, the project successfully improved customer engagement.
